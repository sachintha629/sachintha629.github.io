Flagship AI Innovation

Project Overview

SDOS AI is an advanced artificial intelligence initiative dedicated to maximizing student success. By analyzing individual learning patterns, quiz performances, and conceptual retention curves, the system provides automated, personalized academic recommendations and targeted study interventions.

Traditional classrooms often struggle to address the individual learning pacing of each student. SDOS AI solves this by translating complex mathematical concepts into digestible modules, pinpointing weak areas, and suggesting adaptive practice problem sets.

Adaptive Diagnostic Testing
