About Me

A dedicated software developer, educator, and AI specialist passionate about turning analytical thinking into transformative digital experiences.

Hello, I'm Sachintha Dilshan 👋

I currently serve as a Management Service Officer (IT Division) at the Government of Sri Lanka and am an undergraduate pursuing a Bachelor of Software Engineering (Hons) at the Open University of Sri Lanka.

My foundation in science and analytical thinking began at Mahinda College, Galle (Biology, Chemistry & Physics A/L) and Christ Church Boys' College, Baddegama. Over the years, I have channeled this passion into software architecture, web development, and educational technology.

As an EdTech entrepreneur, I am the founder and owner of SDOS (Sachintha Dilshan Online School), where I have lectured mathematics and developed e-learning solutions for over 2.5 years. Additionally, at sdos.ai, I act as lead developer and AI model trainer, fine-tuning neural algorithms for personalized learning. I am also the co-founder of Randima STUDIO and bring well-rounded professional experience in team coordination and client service from my background at Cargills Ceylon.
